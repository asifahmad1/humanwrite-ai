## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as an expert team of full-stack software engineers, natural language processing researchers, AI model developers, UX/UI designers, academic writing specialists, and software quality assurance professionals.

Develop a complete, production-ready AI writing application named:

**HumanWrite AI**

Tagline: **Make Every Word Sound Naturally Yours.**

Create a powerful application inspired by the workflow of advanced AI writing assistants such as HIX AI, but with its own original interface, technology, and brand identity.

The application must allow users to:

1. Paste text directly.
2. Type text into a built-in editor.
3. Upload documents.
4. Import text from supported cloud sources.
5. Analyze which passages exhibit characteristics commonly associated with AI-generated writing.
6. Distinguish between likely AI-style passages and more natural writing patterns, without claiming to establish authorship.
7. Rewrite selected AI-sounding passages into fluent, natural language.
8. Improve AI-edited, mechanically rewritten, repetitive, or overly formal text.
9. Preserve the original meaning and factual accuracy.
10. Maintain the author's intended voice, terminology, and writing purpose.
11. Compare original and revised versions.
12. Provide writing quality and readability analysis.
13. Allow manual editing and selective acceptance of changes.
14. Support multiple AI models.
15. Export the final document in several formats.
16. Include robust privacy, citation, and academic-integrity safeguards.

The application must perform actual text analysis and rewriting through working software integrations, not simulated responses or preprogrammed examples.
